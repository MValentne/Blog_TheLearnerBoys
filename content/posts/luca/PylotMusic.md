# Intro

For some context, there's a band called Pylot and they make electronic synthwavey music. Ive liked the music they put out for a while, my brother showed them to me like 5-6 years ago and I have not stopped listening to them.

They released a new LP (LP = album too short to be a album) and I just wanted to share my thoughts.

The links to every song and album are on the names of each respective one, I expect you to listen to each one, if you like it or not is your deal but LISTEN TO THEM.

# [The Carbon Ghost](https://open.spotify.com/album/7h4hx7S1vSnoRdMyD0SRrV?si=qN2rc-INTwmgovRqqEXBGg) 

Only the brightest have deducted from context clues that the LP is called *The Carbon Ghost*, Ill talk about what i think about each song in order and then ill head into overall thoughts.

## [The Black Loop](https://open.spotify.com/track/7oeh1vnMkcemWHYHn6WoYN?si=2fabb9acf24f4217) 8/10

This song is very Pylot, no lyrics just pure beeps and boops, jokes aside this song is pretty clean and does what Pylot does best. Although a good song I cant help but think it could have been more but I do think its unfair to judge an artists work by his past work, sometimes the stars align for certain things and then they arent fully aligned for others. This song is a jam, dont get me wrong, its just missing something that some older tracks have. All in all its Pylots fault for making BANGERS before, shame shame shame.

## [Splitlane Echo](https://open.spotify.com/track/7M2VnDvD9lLgzJ307HW8BA?si=1764bda2e0434c6c) 9/10

This takes on a slower tempo compared to the last song, but I think the Mix of sounds all work in tandem to deliver a cool song. Pylots done slower songs before and i think this one is a very solid one. very cool, very enjoyable. Not much to say its just good.

## [The Carbon Ghost](https://open.spotify.com/track/4YAZPGLkQpwsfFVPhavUq9?si=39bbe24d79214517) 10/10

The title song, the big kahoona (idk how to spell that) does it live up to the hype? Ofcourse it does. Right off the gate it starts quick and strong, the only song in the LP with lyrics and I think they are pretty cool. Especially when it says "CARBON GHOST" in the background, something about that just tickles my brain. Pylots music has always captured the feeling of Driving a motorcycle at high speeds through a long highway at night, in other words high octane. It might seem obvious since the artist is called Pylot and if you look at the picture its a motorcycle helmet, They knew what they were doing and its a b-b-b-b-banger in my humble opinion. Honestly at first I was iffy on the voice but it grew on me and I saw that it fits this track perfectly.

## [Heart Engine](https://open.spotify.com/track/5AGYyMCAZ8mJ0J3FA6Ejlk?si=cf8de8b999d94970) 10/10

If the second song was slow, this one is even slower and I dont say that to detract points, I like whatever as long as it sounds good. This song has the tempo of a slow heartbeat, it helps that there is a literal heartbeat playing throughout the song. This one is my second favorite in the LP I think its one of the better very slow songs Pylots ever made.

# Final Thoughts

The LP has one last song but its a version of carbon ghost without the lyrics and it just butchers the song, they didnt even keep the "CARBON GHOST" it repeats which is INTEGRAL to that song.

Now onto my final thoughts, This LP is very solid. Its a shame that its short and has only a few but from what ive seen pylot works better in short bursts rather than a full length album, its like they get in the zone when its a few songs. I say this because they had done a full album before this and it honestly sucked hot ass, its like the soul of Pylot was sucked out. Im very happy to see that they have been resurrected. Overall this album hit alot of spots I thought pylot would not hit again, thats why I give this album a solid 9/10.

Thats about it see ya.

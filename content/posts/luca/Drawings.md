# Intro

I dont consider myself an artist but I recently started doing little drawings in this neat notebook I got. Its quite fun doing these drawings and I just wanted to share them here.

## Drawing

I started doing these little guys at school, It first was me just passing the time and scribbling in the edges of the pages of my school notebook. I liked making them look like prehistoric cave paintings (idk why it just seemed cool) and I have no pictures of these since those pages are now lost (thank my mother for that). I developed a sort of story for these guys where they are just chilling and a shape comes to them and they worship it. 

Now the little story is kind of the same but it just has a little more meaning, its nothing new but i think its a cool subject. ![IMG-7142.jpg](https://i.postimg.cc/vZ5jVqPr/IMG-7142.jpg)

It just tells the age old story of humanity gaining intelligence is our biggest curse and our biggest blessing all in one. Animals can do harm to one another but that is just life and its all instinct they have no morality its just survive. Humans want to survive and we have these instincts but they inherently crash with our conscience leading to us being the only beings that we know of capable of evil and good.

Sadly the negative tends to triumph most of the time because usually its the easier path and its become easier in the path humanity has been set down. The reason the little guys at first have no eyes and have basic shapes for heads is just that, humans before we had the weight of conscience.

Now I have not done more drawings showing the beauty in conscience so im not the person to say "we should just all die off", im actually very positive regarding my thoughts on us as a species, that does not mean I cant see reality and I see the world we live in.

The last drawing I made was kind of just scribbles with no meaning except me trying new things out. When I draw more ill be sure to share it here. ![IMG-7143.jpg](https://i.postimg.cc/hvww9HWw/IMG-7143.jpg)

Thats about it, see ya.

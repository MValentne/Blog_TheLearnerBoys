# Intro

This happened almost a month ago from when im posting this. Brenda (my girlfriend) had come to visit and she brought her camara for the purpose of taking cool pictures in some location, so I got in contact with my aunts husband which we call *bebbeo* to plan a little excursion somewhere cool since he is a professional photographer. 

We came to the conclusion that this trip would be held in *El Bosque De Chapultepec*,so we made a date. The day arrived and so did we in his apartment, the members of this adventure were Brenda, Sebastian (my brother), Bebbeo, Leondario (my little cousin and son of bebbeo) and myself.

## Adventure

The destination was a short walk from his house, we set out and bought some water and snacks in a local OXXO. We got there and bebbeo served as our guide in telling us what cool stuff we might like to see. first we went to this big hole in the ground that was a old sewer system, which was "ok", was not really all that interesting.

Now as the name suggests this place is a giant urban forest, what does this mean? it is surrounded by a city and fun fact, it is the largest urban forest in the world. We explored around in the forest and brenda took quite a handful of pictures of stuff, ranging from trees, grass or the critter that inhabit such things. Me with all my artistic vision took photos and videos of squirrels, I will share my favourite out of the bunch ![P1100797.jpg](https://i.postimg.cc/nrqYzXyZ/P1100797.jpg) 

pretty cool, no? Im no pro but I dont think I did too bad with a camara as old as I am (20 years).

After all that we went to go see a fountain but before we reached that bebbeo told us about these trees whichs names I have forgotten that grow only in mexico and look pretty cool, this one in particular had a name like *El General* and was a pretty cool big tree. ![P1100800.jpg](https://i.postimg.cc/j5cRrc5r/P1100800.jpg)

big ass tree right there. I liked how the very top of it looked.

The fountain was right next to the tree and we spent quite some time here because Brenda was taking some crazy pictures of stairs, they came out quite good, I dont have these pictures but surely she will share them at some point. I took pictures of the fountain but I really only liked one. ![P1100806.jpg](https://i.postimg.cc/x1v77C8t/P1100806.jpg)

I think it looked cool. There was this other guy with a camara and he had a giant camara lens which was cool and when he was done taking pictures he rode off on his bike.

## The End

Once we got back we all started talking about random stuff but we listened to music on my ipod that we connected to a speaker which was pretty cool. We showed him music from bands we like (The GOATS, the family crest, whoever reads this check them out they are incredible), we did this for like 4 hours. Then my mother came to pick us up and we went home.

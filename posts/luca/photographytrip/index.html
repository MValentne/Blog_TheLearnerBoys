Photography trip

Author: Luca
Keywords: Fotos, viaje
Categories: Fotografia

Intro

This happened almost a month ago from when im posting this. Brenda (my girlfriend) had come to visit and she brought her camara for the purpose of taking cool pictures in some location, so I got in contact with my aunts husband which we call bebbeo to plan a little excursion somewhere cool since he is a professional photographer.

We came to the conclusion that this trip would be held in El Bosque De Chapultepec,so we made a date. The day arrived and so did we in his apartment, the members of this adventure were Brenda, Sebastian (my brother), Bebbeo, Leondario (my little cousin and son of bebbeo) and myself.

Adventure

The destination was a short walk from his house, we set out and bought some water and snacks in a local OXXO. We got there and bebbeo served as our guide in telling us what cool stuff we might like to see. first we went to this big hole in the ground that was a old sewer system, which was “ok”, was not really all that interesting.

Now as the name suggests this place is a giant urban forest, what does this mean? it is surrounded by a city and fun fact, it is the largest urban forest in the world. We explored around in the forest and brenda took quite a handful of pictures of stuff, ranging from trees, grass or the critter that inhabit such things. Me with all my artistic vision took photos and videos of squirrels, I will share my favourite out of the bunch

pretty cool, no? Im no pro but I dont think I did too bad with a camara as old as I am (20 years).

After all that we went to go see a fountain but before we reached that bebbeo told us about these trees whichs names I have forgotten that grow only in mexico and look pretty cool, this one in particular had a name like El General and was a pretty cool big tree.

big ass tree right there. I liked how the very top of it looked.

The fountain was right next to the tree and we spent quite some time here because Brenda was taking some crazy pictures of stairs, they came out quite good, I dont have these pictures but surely she will share them at some point. I took pictures of the fountain but I really only liked one.

I think it looked cool. There was this other guy with a camara and he had a giant camara lens which was cool and when he was done taking pictures he rode off on his bike.

Our next spot was a very tranquil pond filled with fish, duck and this big magestic bird which I think is a swan but dont quote me on it, That swan was quite elusive, he did not want his picture taken. I took a few of the ducks and a couple of the swan. ^ Two ducks just hanging around.

^ Just one duck, but I really liked this one. The ripples in the water and the ducks reflection looked quite cool.

^ The cool big bird, I took the first picture and he auto left.

There was a bigger pond next to the duck one where people could rent boats and row around, so we got one and me and bebbeo were in charge of rowing. It was very fun, I had never rowed a boat before. It started to rain While we were still in the boat which just added a cinematic effect to it. I dont have any pictures of our vessel so you will have to use your imagination. The picture below was later in the day but it was in this body of water where we rowed the boat. Something just tickled my pickle so, snap.

It was getting late and I found the mother of all sticks, it was GIANT and shaped like a wizard staff, sadly I dont have iamges of it but Brenda does. We went to this big wall fountain that was quite long and I saw this wolf head and a tree next to it and I had a vision.

Pretty cool how its peaking its head out.

It was getting late so we started walking back and on our way back were a myriad of little shops and I took some pics of a couple because of how much raw aura they possessed. ^ Perchance Mario Vs Sonic.

^ Tricksy little bart.

While we walked Leondario caught a glimpse of some super raw plushies and I bought him one.

Its funny because it looks like the design is based off the void eyes.

The End

We left and it started pouring cats and dogs, luckily Bebbeos apartment was close but we still got very wet. Once we got back we all started talking about random stuff but we listened to music on my ipod that we connected to a speaker which was pretty cool. We showed him music from bands we like (The GOATS, the family crest, whoever reads this check them out they are incredible), we did this for like 4 hours. Then my mother came to pick us up and we went home.

All in all pretty fun and cool day. Bye Bye
